Privacy

YOU vs 166,691 — last updated 2026-09-11

This is a small browser game. It does not ask for an account, an email address, a phone number or a password, and it is not used to build a profile about you beyond what is described on this page.

What is public

  • Your nickname. You choose it yourself and it is shown to everyone on the global leaderboard.
  • Your scores. Each finished game is stored with its score and a few gameplay statistics (escapes caused, closest approach, Giant Fiber peak, elapsed time, attempts used). Your best score is shown publicly next to your nickname.

Please do not use a nickname that identifies you or anyone else if you would rather stay anonymous.

Account identifier

To keep your scores attached to you between visits, the game signs you in anonymously through Supabase. Supabase issues a random account id for your browser and stores a session token in this browser. That id is not linked to an email address, a phone number or any real-world identity, and we do not connect it to anything else.

Stored in your browser

The game keeps a few small values in this browser's localStorage: your chosen language, your local best score, the number of games you have finished, and the Supabase session token described above. Clearing your browser data removes them; you will then get a new anonymous id, and your old scores stay on the leaderboard under your old nickname.

Hosting and network

Loading the page involves the static host that serves it and, if the leaderboard is enabled, requests to Supabase. Those services process the usual technical data any web request produces, such as an IP address, in order to deliver the response and protect against abuse.

Advertising

Ads are currently not enabled. If advertising is enabled in a future version, it will use Google's H5 Games Ad Placement API, and Google may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads. You can review Google's practices at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. This page will be updated when that happens.

Analytics

No third-party analytics product is loaded. The game contains internal event hooks (for example "game started" or "score submitted") that currently do nothing beyond optional local debug output.

Removing your data

If you want your nickname and scores deleted, contact the site owner with your nickname and it will be removed.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
